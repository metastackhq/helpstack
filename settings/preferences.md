# Profile Settings
---

In the **Profile Settings** you can customize and manage your personal details.

### Editable Information
- **Profile Image** - upload or change your avatar.  
- **Display Name** - update your visible name.  
- **Username** - customize your username.
- **Email** - update your account email.  
- **Competitive Level** - change your in-game level (see [Competitive Level](#competitive-level)).

### Profile Details
- **Description** - a short bio or personal note.  
- **Discord** - link your Discord handle for communication.  
- **Region** - set your geographical region.  
- **Spoken Languages** - list the languages you communicate in.  

### External Links
- Add up to **10 external links** (e.g., social media, streaming channels, or personal pages).

## Competitive Level

Your **competitive level** is your own estimate of how experienced you are at Counter-Strike. It helps us shape Metastack around your needs.

You choose it once while [creating your account](/getting-started/account), and you can change it at any time.

| Level            | Pick this if...                                                                                              |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Beginner**     | You started playing recently, have less than 100 hours in the game, and want to improve fast.                |
| **Casual**       | You play occasionally, have a few hundred hours on record, and want to level up your game.                   |
| **Advanced**     | You're an experienced player, compete at a higher level, and strive to win every match.                      |
| **Semi-Pro**     | You've played on a team, taken part in some competitions, and you're aiming for the pro level.              |
| **Professional** | You're on a professional team, get paid to play, and need every advantage possible.                          |

:::info PRIVATE
Your answer is entirely subjective and private. It is not shown on your profile, and Metastack doesn't share it with anyone.
:::

### Changing Your Level
1. Go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Next to **Competitive Level**, click **Edit**.
3. Pick a new level from the list and click **Save**.

:::tip REFERRALS
Choosing a level is the last step of signing up. If you joined through a [referral link](/getting-started/referral-program), this is the step that earns you and your friend the reward.
:::
