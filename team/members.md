# Team Members
---

The **Team Members** tab provides an overview of all members in your team and their assigned ranks.  
From here, you can see who is part of your team and what level of access they have.  

## Members Overview
In the Members tab, you can view:  
- **List of all team members**.  
- **Rank of each member** (Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer).  

## Managing Members
To manage ranks or permissions:  
1. Open **Settings** from the profile menu in the top-right corner.  
2. Under **Team Settings**, open **Members**.  
3. Locate the member you want to edit.  
4. Select their new rank from the dropdown next to their name.  

:::info
Only **Owners** and **Admins** can update member ranks.
- **Owners** can assign the Admin, Member, and Viewer ranks.  
- **Admins** can switch members between Member and Viewer. They cannot change the Owner or other Admins.  
:::

## Ranks
There are 4 ranks available:  
- **Owner** – has all permissions.  
- **Admin** – can manage team settings, invite new members, and has full access to content.  
- **Member** – can fully access team content, but cannot manage team settings or invite members.  
- **Viewer** – read-only access; can only view team content.  

For a detailed breakdown of what each rank can do, see the [Permissions](./permissions.md) page.  

## Inviting Members
New members join a team through its **invite link**.

1. Go to **Settings → Team Settings → Members**.  
2. Make sure **Enable joining via link** is turned on.  
3. Copy the invite link and send it to the people you want to invite.  

Everyone who opens the link and logs in joins the team as a **Member**. You can change their rank afterwards.

- Turn **Enable joining via link** off to stop new members from joining.  
- Click **Generate a New Link** to replace the invite link. The old link stops working.  

:::info
Only **Owners** and **Admins** can see the invite link and change these settings. They can also open it from **Team → Invite Members** in the left sidebar.
:::

## Removing Members and Leaving a Team
In **Settings → Team Settings → Members**, open the menu next to a member:  
- **Remove** - removes the member from the team. Owners can remove anyone. Admins can remove Members and Viewers.  
- **Leave Team** - shown next to your own name. Admins, Members, and Viewers can leave a team at any time.  

The **Owner** cannot leave the team. To step away, hand ownership over to another member first, or delete the team.
