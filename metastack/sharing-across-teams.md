# Copying Across Teams
---

## When to Use

Use **Copy to** for copying a **Strat, Nade, Combo, or Keyword** to another team or your personal space.

**Copy to** leaves the original where it is and adds a copy to where you want it.

## How to Use

- Find the Strat, Nade, Combo, or Keyword you want to copy, either in its list or on its own page
- Open the action menu and choose **Copy to**
- Select where to copy it in the dropdown: your personal space, or up to 5 teams at once
- Confirm

## Combos and Their Nades

When you copy a **Combo**, its Nades are copied to the destination as well, unless they already exist there. The Combo and its Nades remain in the original space.

Copying an individual Nade leaves the Combos it belongs to unchanged.

## Permissions and Availability

**Copy to** appears only when you belong to more than one team where you are not a Viewer.

To use it, you need permission for creating content.
- **Owners, Admins, and Members** can create content.
- **Viewers** can view content but cannot create it.

See [Team Permissions](../team/permissions.md) for the full breakdown.
